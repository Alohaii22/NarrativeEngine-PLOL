What a campaign needs

Required
World lore (.md): the setting, factions, places, people, magic. Every bundled world includes one.
Recommended
GM rules (.md): how the GM plays. A good default is chosen for you. Opening prompt: how the story starts, often with character creation.
Optional
Loot table (.json), character cards (SillyTavern .png/.json) for extra NPCs, and your hero sheet.

Not sure? Pick a bundled world and press Create & Play. Everything else is optional.
